Master’s student · CUHK-Shenzhen

I am a master's student in Computer and Information Engineering at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. My research focuses on embodied AI, multi-robot collaboration, and efficient long-horizon task planning.

I study how robots can turn incomplete observations into reliable plans: connecting language-based reasoning, symbolic task models, and coordinated action in the physical world.
